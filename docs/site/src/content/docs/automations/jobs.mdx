Anything the panel does that takes longer than a page load runs as a
[job](/docs/reference/glossary/#job): creating a site, a backup, an update, a command. Each job
keeps a log you can read while it runs, and ends as succeeded, failed or canceled.

<Screenshot name="jobs" alt="Automations → All jobs with running, failed and finished jobs" />

## How jobs run

A site has one active job at a time. A second change to a busy site is refused with
`409 job_conflict`. Each server also runs one job at a time, so jobs for sites on the same server
wait for each other. A site move holds both of its servers.

Some work runs in a [lane](/docs/reference/glossary/#lane) of its own, beside the server's other
jobs:

| Lane | What runs there | At a time |
|---|---|---|
| `offsite:<server>` | Offsite copies | One per server |
| `exec:<server>` | WP-CLI commands, shell commands and REST API requests | One per server |
| `scan:<server>` | Malware scans and plugin zip checks | One per server |
| `housekeeping` | The nightly housekeeping | One |
| `backup-delete` | <Since v="0.3.0-beta.2" /> Deleting several backups at once | One |

## Find a job

Open **Automations → All jobs**. Each row shows the job, its site, a summary, its status, who
started it and how long it took. The summary masks anything that looks like a password.

- The search box finds `#123` as that job. Other text matches a summary, site, error, job type or
  job name, so "inventory" finds the scans.
- The status chips combine, such as **Failed** and **Canceled** together.
- **Job**, **Site**, **Server**, **Triggered by** and **When** narrow the list further.

The filters are part of the page's address, so you can bookmark a filtered list or share it.

## See who started it

| **Triggered by** | Shown as | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **In the panel** | The admin's username | Someone clicked something in the panel |
| **API** | `API key "deploy-script"` | A request made with that key |
| **AI apps (MCP)** | `Claude via MCP (approved by sam)` or `API key "deploy-script" via MCP` | An AI app's tool call |
| **Schedule** | The schedule's name | A schedule fired on its own. **Run now** is credited to whoever pressed it |
| **System** | Panel | Follow-up work the panel queued itself |

## Follow a job

A job's page shows its **Details**, **What this job does** and, once it ends, its **Result**. The
**Log** follows the job live. Show only **Warnings & errors**, search it, **Copy** it or choose
**Download .log**. On a failed job, **Show in log** jumps to the first error.

## Cancel a job

Choose **Cancel job**. A queued job is canceled at once. A running job stops at its next safe step,
once you confirm with **Cancel the job**. Most jobs keep what they already did. Creating a site and
moving one undo their own steps instead.

A WP-CLI command, shell command or REST API request that has started cannot be interrupted. It runs
to its end or to its time limit.

## Stuck and timed-out jobs

Each job type has a time limit, listed in [Job types](/docs/reference/job-types/). A job past its
limit fails with "Job timed out after" a number of minutes, and stops at its next safe step.

A job that was running when the panel restarted fails with "Interrupted by panel restart". A site
it was creating or deleting is marked as an error. Run the job again.

## Keep finished jobs

Finished jobs are kept for 90 days by default. Then the nightly
[housekeeping](/docs/reference/glossary/#housekeeping) removes them with their logs. Change the
period under the list, at **Finished jobs are kept for**.

## Limits

- A long job delays the other jobs on its server.
- The panel runs at most eight jobs at the same time, across all servers.
- A job stopped partway, by a cancel or a timeout, can leave its work half done. Its log shows how
  far it got.
- An interrupted job is not resumed.
