**Reminders** tracks renewals, support deadlines and maintenance. Its badge is red for due or overdue, amber for upcoming warnings.

<Screenshot name="reminders" alt="A reminder checklist grouped by due and upcoming dates" />

## Add a reminder

Choose **New reminder**, enter a title and date, then **Save reminder**.
**Warn days before** takes comma-separated days, such as `30, 7, 0`. Due dates always warn and use the panel timezone.

**Recurring** repeats every number of days, weeks, months or years. Templates cover licenses, restore tests, contracts and bills.
Check the circle to complete a reminder. Repeats advance beyond today; missed occurrences produce one warning. Monthly repeats preserve their original day.
Open **Completed** and uncheck an item to reopen it.

Click a title to edit. The **…** menu has **Snooze**, **Edit** and **Delete**.
**Snooze** hides warnings until tomorrow, next week or a chosen date.

## Track renewals and support dates

In **New reminder**, choose **PHP end of life** or **Server OS end of life** under **Kind**.
Pick a PHP version or server; its support date fills automatically. **Check sources** loads server dates.
Already tracked items are omitted. Unknown versions need a one-time reminder.
**Cloudflare token expiry** uses the same picker.

<Screenshot name="reminders-eol" alt="Adding a server OS end-of-life reminder with its support date filled in" />

**Suggestions** also lists domains, PHP versions, operating systems and expiring tokens. Choose **Add** to track one.
Source checks are cached daily.

Domain dates come from RDAP. Without a published date, enter one manually and set the renewal term.
Registry statuses still refresh for manually dated domains, without replacing the entered date.
Marking a manual renewal done advances its date by that term.
**Awaiting confirmation** waits three days for a changed registry date. It does not renew the domain.
Undo mistakes with the clock circle or **Undo done** in the **…** menu; the date stays unchanged.
Without a changed date, the usual status returns. Renewals made without clicking Done are detected too.

Registry auto-renew grace does not confirm payment. Hold, redemption and deletion stay red, even during snooze.
**Domains** shows expiry dates, registrars and registry status.

PHP follows security support; OS dates cover Ubuntu standard maintenance and Debian LTS.
Ubuntu dates mark the published final month’s first day. Upgrades resolve old support reminders.

## Receive email or subscribe

Enable **Email reminder** and choose the alert address or a user with confirmed email.
Digests arrive daily after 09:00 panel time; overdue reminders repeat weekly. Failed delivery retries after 15 minutes.

**Calendar feed** creates a secret subscription link. Anyone with it can read titles, dates and notes.
**Replace link** invalidates the old link; **Revoke link** disables it.

## Limits

- Registry dates may differ from registrar billing dates. No WHOIS or registrar APIs.
- Domain lookups run daily. Failures keep the last date.
- Disable lookups in **Settings → Monitoring → Look up domain expiry dates**.
- **Schedules** pauses reminder processing and domain lookups separately.
- Paid support extensions are not detected.
- Calendars show active reminders' next occurrence. Calendar apps control refresh intervals.
- API and MCP changes require Full access.
