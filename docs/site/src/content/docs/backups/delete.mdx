Deleting a backup removes it everywhere: its files on the server and its offsite copies. A deleted
backup cannot be brought back, so the panel always asks first.

## Delete one backup

1. Find the backup on the site's **Backups** tab or under **Backups → All backups**.
2. Choose **Delete**. The dialog says which remote copies go with it.
3. To free the server's disk but keep the remote copies, switch on **Keep the remote copies**. The
   backup then stays listed as **remote only**.
4. Choose **Delete**.

A destination with **Retention is managed by the provider** switched on keeps its copy, and the
panel says so. If a remote copy cannot be removed, the backup is kept, so nothing stays in a bucket
without the panel knowing.

## Delete several backups

<Since v="0.3.0-beta.2" /> On **Backups → All backups**, tick the backups to delete. The box at the
top of the list ticks the whole page. A bar then shows how many are selected. Choose **Delete** on
it, type `delete`, and confirm.

With a filter on, ticking the whole page also offers **Select all**: every backup the filters match,
on every page. Set **Kind** to **Before an update**, for example, to clear those out on every site.
The selection is fixed when you make it, and holds at most 5,000 backups. Without a filter, **Select
all** is not offered.

## Delete all of a site's backups

<Since v="0.3.0-beta.2" /> On the site's **Backups** tab, choose **Bulk delete**. It opens **Backups →
All backups** filtered to that site. Tick the page, choose **Select all** if the list runs over
several pages, then **Delete**. Type the site's name to confirm.

## What a bulk deletion does

<Since v="0.3.0-beta.2" /> One **Delete backups** job works through the selection, oldest first,
without holding up any server's sites. Each backup goes the way **Delete** takes one. Rows the job
has not reached yet say **Deleting…**, and cannot be restored, fetched or copied. A backup another
job is using is left alone, and the job then ends as failed, naming it.

## What deleting a site does with its backups

When you delete a site, **Take a final backup first (kept until you delete it)** is on by default.
The final backup and the site's other backups stay after the site is gone, under **Deleted sites**
on **Backups → All backups**. Scheduled ones still follow retention. Deleting the last complete
backup of a deleted site asks you to type its name.

<Since v="0.3.0-beta.2" /> To take the site's backups along, switch on **Delete its existing backups
too** as well. Once the site is gone, a **Delete backups** job removes them, remote copies
included. The final backup is not among them, so with both switches on, the site leaves exactly one
backup behind. With only the second switch on, nothing of the site is left.

<Since v="0.3.0-beta.3" /> **Remove from panel** on a [site hosted elsewhere](/docs/sites/external/)
takes no final backup. Its backups stay, unless **Also delete its backups** is on.

## Limits

- A deleted backup cannot be recovered, from the server or from a destination the panel cleared.
- A backup cannot be deleted while it is being written or uploaded, or while a job for its site may
  be reading it.
- A destination that manages its own retention keeps its copies. Remove them with the provider's
  tools.
- Deleting a backup's local files with **Keep the remote copies** leaves it restorable only after
  **Fetch back**.
