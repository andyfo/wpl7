Three entries sit at the bottom of the sidebar. **Appearance** sets how the panel looks in your
browser, **Support** is where to get help, and **About** says what this install is.

## Change the theme and accent color

Open **Appearance** and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, which follows your device. Under
**Theme color**, pick one of seven accent colors. Blue is the default.

The choice is kept in your browser and applies to every open tab of the panel at once. Each browser
keeps its own.

## Get help on the Support page

<Screenshot name="support" alt="The Support page with the community and Enterprise cards" />

| Section | What it offers |
|---|---|
| **Community support** | The community forum, where people running WPL7 help each other. **Open community forum** goes there |
| **Enterprise package** | Hotfixes, priority email support and a dedicated engineer, for businesses that run WPL7 in production. **Explore Enterprise** opens its page on wpl7.com |
| **Found a bug?** | **GitHub issues**, or a private report for a security problem |

## See what this install is

Open **About**, or choose the version in the header. Three cards describe the install:

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Version** | Version, commit, channel, the last update check and whether a newer version is out |
| **Machine** | The panel's server: system, kernel, CPU, memory, uptime and Docker |
| **Network** | Its address, reverse DNS, the panel's domain, Node and how long the panel has been up |

**Check for updates** asks for a new version at once. Applying one is on
[Updating WPL7](/docs/panel/updating/).

<Screenshot name="about" alt="The About page with the version, machine and network cards" />

## Send feedback

**Send feedback** on the About page offers three kinds of message:

- **Something is broken** and **Something is missing** open a new GitHub issue in your browser,
  under your own account. Nothing is sent from the panel.
- **A question or an idea** is sent by the panel to the community forum. Nothing is public until
  you post it there yourself.

**Include what this install is** adds the build and the machine, without addresses, domains or site
names. Read a pasted log for passwords and customer domains before you send it.

## Limits

- The theme and accent belong to the browser, not to your account. They do not follow you to
  another device.
- The **Machine** card describes the panel's own server, not the other servers.
- The Enterprise package is a premium service offered on wpl7.com, not inside the panel.
