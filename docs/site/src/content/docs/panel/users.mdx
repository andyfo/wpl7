Everyone who signs in to the panel has an account of their own, listed under **Users**. Every
[admin](/docs/reference/glossary/#admin) can do everything. The
[owner](/docs/reference/glossary/#owner), the account the install created, differs only in that
nobody else may change or remove it.

<Screenshot name="users" alt="Users with the owner and two admins, one with two-factor authentication on" />

## Add an admin

1. Open **Users** and choose **Add admin**.
2. Enter a **Username** and a first **Password** of at least 10 characters.
3. Choose **Add admin**.

The new admin can change the password on their own page. A username that differs from a taken one
only in letter case is refused.

## Change a username, password or recovery email

Open the account from **Users**. Every change asks for **Your password**: the password of whoever
makes the change, never the account's own.

- **Change username** signs nobody out.
- On your own account, **Change password** signs out every other browser signed in as you. On a
  colleague's, **Set password** ends every session they have.
- **Send confirmation link** sets a recovery email, where "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page
  sends its link. The address counts once its link is followed, within 24 hours.

Only the owner can change the owner's account.

## Set up two-factor authentication

1. On your own page, under **Two-factor authentication**, choose **Set up** and enter your password.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, type the code it shows, and choose **Turn it on**.
3. Save the ten recovery codes, which are shown only once, and choose **I have saved them**.

Turning it on signs out your other sessions. From then on, signing in asks for a code from the app,
or one of the recovery codes. **New recovery codes** replaces the set.

Only the person holding the phone can set it up. When an admin loses the phone and the codes,
another admin can choose **Turn off** on their page. The owner's can only be turned off by the owner.

<Screenshot name="user-detail" alt="An admin's page with two-factor authentication on" />

## Remove an admin

Choose **Remove** in the list, or **Remove admin** on their page, and type their username to
confirm. Their sessions end at once. AI apps they approved are disconnected too. API keys are not
affected. The owner cannot be removed, and you cannot remove yourself.

## Limits

- There are no lesser roles. Every admin controls the whole panel and every server, terminal
  included. Give an account only to someone you would trust with root.
- Two-factor authentication guards the browser sign-in only. API keys are a separate credential.
- An owner who forgot the password and has no recovery email, or lost both the phone and the
  recovery codes, needs a shell on the server to get back in.
